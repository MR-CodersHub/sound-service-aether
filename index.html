ARCHITECTURAL METHODOLOGY

From First Conversation to First Showing.

Our white-glove four-stage installation framework guarantees pristine acoustic performance, seamless smart integration, and zero architectural compromise.

01

Discover

Understand your room, preferences, budget, and entertainment goals through an on-site architectural walkthrough and acoustic baseline analysis.

02

Design

Plan the system, equipment, placement, acoustics, and integration with 3D ray-tracing simulations, sightline modeling, and custom CAD schematics.

03

Install

Professionally install and configure your selected equipment with decoupled vibration dampening, structured concealment, and pristine cable management.

04

Calibrate

Fine-tune the picture, sound, and system controls for your space using multi-point reference microphones and optical colorimetry spectrometers.
