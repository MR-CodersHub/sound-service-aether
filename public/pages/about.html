OUR STORY

Engineers, Dreamers & Lifelong Audiophiles.

For over fifteen years, Aether Cinematics has designed and installed private cinemas and audio systems for people who care deeply about how their homes feel and sound.

HOW IT BEGAN

A Single Room That Changed Everything.

In 2011 our founder converted a tired basement into a reference listening room for a friend. Word travelled quickly. Within a year we were designing dedicated cinemas for clients who had been disappointed by generic packages and exposed wiring.

Today we are a fourteen-person studio of acoustic engineers, projection specialists and automation programmers. We remain deliberately independent, so we can specify the right component for each room rather than whatever a supplier is pushing that quarter.

We believe a great installation should feel inevitable, as though the room was always meant to sound and look this way.
