1. Introduction

Aether Cinematics ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who visits our website, contacts our studio or commissions our installation services. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it and how we keep it safe.

By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the site.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly, information gathered automatically as you browse, and information from trusted partners.

  • Information you give us: name, email address, phone number, project location, property details and any message content you submit through our forms or by email.
  • Information collected automatically: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited and referring URLs, gathered through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Information from partners: referrals and, where relevant, architectural or design partners working on your project.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information to deliver and improve our services, including to:

  • Respond to enquiries and arrange consultations;
  • Prepare quotations, proposals and project documentation;
  • Deliver installations, programming and aftercare;
  • Send service updates and, where you have opted in, marketing communications;
  • Meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses essential cookies to remember preferences such as your chosen theme and language direction. We may also use analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Installation and logistics partners strictly as required to deliver your project;
  • Manufacturers for warranty registration where you have requested it;
  • Service providers such as payment processors, email platforms and analytics tools under confidentiality obligations;
  • Authorities where we are legally required to disclose information.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to object to marketing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we will respond within the timescales required by law.

7. Retention & Security

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy and to satisfy legal requirements. We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit, access controls and staff training, to protect your information against unauthorised access or loss.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be highlighted on this page with a revised "last updated" date. We encourage you to review it periodically.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact our privacy team at privacy@aethercinematics.com or write to Aether Cinematics, 9440 Wilshire Blvd, Beverly Hills, CA 90212.
